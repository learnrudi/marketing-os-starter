## Status values

- `Draft` — captured but not verified.
- `Candidate` — evidence suggests the claim, but wording, scope, or permission still requires review.
- `Needs review` — evidence exists and approval is pending.
- `Approved` — may be used within the stated approved use.
- `Expired` — must be revalidated before use.
- `Rejected` — must not be used.
