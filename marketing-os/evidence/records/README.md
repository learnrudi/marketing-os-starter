# Evidence Records

Create one provenance-aware record per evidence item using `../../templates/evidence-record.md`.

Evidence may include:

- owned operational or delivery data;
- approved case studies and customer outcomes;
- reviews, surveys, support feedback, or interview findings;
- public comments and campaign responses;
- external research, benchmarks, awards, or certifications.

Public availability does not automatically grant permission to reuse an identifiable comment as a testimonial. Preserve the distinction between aggregated customer insight and attributable promotional proof.
