# Research

Research contains evidence-backed understanding of the market and the problems marketing may address.

```text
research/
├── problems/
├── market/
├── competitors/
└── source-material/
```

- `problems/` contains explicit customer or market problem hypotheses and their validation state.
- `market/` contains category, trend, demand, and environmental research.
- `competitors/` contains sourced research on alternatives and competing approaches.
- `source-material/` preserves original notes, documents, exports, screenshots, and other inputs.

Separate observed findings from interpretation. Preserve direct source links, publication dates, capture dates, and scope. Mark unknowns as `[NEEDS RESEARCH]`; do not turn plausible assumptions into customer facts.

Business problems and company outcomes belong under `../../business/objectives/`. Campaign-specific framing belongs inside the applicable campaign brief.
